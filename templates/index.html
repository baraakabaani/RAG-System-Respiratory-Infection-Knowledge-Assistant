Failure Analysis
PERSISTENT
Cross-Disease Contamination — Q09
Chronic cough + fever + night sweats (TB symptoms) returns COVID-19 documents across ALL configurations. The embedding model treats shared symptom vocabulary equally across disease domains.
💡 Future work: pre-retrieval disease-domain intent classifier
FIXED
Wrong-Document Provenance — Q03, Q08, Q10
Baseline retrieved influenza PDF at Rank 1 for a COVID-19 corticosteroid query. Fixed by Hybrid RRF (Q03) and Cross-Encoder reranking (Q08, Q10).
PARTIAL
Incomplete Grounding — Q03 Dexamethasone Dosage
The 6mg dexamethasone dose is in a separate WHO section from the recommendation. No single chunk contains all expected terms. Grounding@Q03 = 0.00 across all systems.
💡 Future work: table-aware extraction + multi-chunk synthesis
